I just wanted to share a small rant on my recent (literally today's) experience with color schemes.

I have been, for a very long time now, been using the nordic neovim theme and I love it, but I also fucking hate it already. I am tired to look at the same half saturated color all the time, so I decided to move to some new horizons. Nightfox looked kinda good after 20 minutes of trying to find one that I half liked. It had its quirks but nothing that couldn't be fixed.

2 hours later and I have now learned exactly the thing that stopped me from just doing my own theme six months ago... hl groups. And all because I simply couldn't cope with the fact that Nightfox.nvim decided that keywords shall also encompass most of the C preprocessor to the point where I had to choose between seeing either my beautiful (I hate it) wall of "#elif" being purple or having "typedef struct" being red... I can't.

This is how, today, I learned that I never wanted a new theme, I just wanted new colors. I will never doubt again the nordic.nvim API, because I obviously didn't know what I had until I lost it.

The End.
