I have literally watched all of your videos today and, although I disagree with some stuff, nothing was inherently wrong about your approach. However, that explanation is lacking substance.

Yes, non-vibe coders will be slower, yes, the portfolio will be less impressive, but no, the traditional one will not be memorising syntax, because syntax is the first thing that stops being important in programming.

Vibe coding is a developer artifact, not an engineering one. The capacity to build proper large scale infrastructure is not available to a vibe coder. I have had to spend hours fixing vibe code because their implementation is wrong from the very beginning in an extremely subtle way. Their work might be faster than mine, but I am the one who has to fix their implementation from the start, and that often means deleting everything they had and putting what is salvageable on mine, because AI always has Jr. Programmer mistakes.

If you don't know how to do things line by line yourself, to vibe code something will only become the source of bugs in the long run.

AI will not always give you the best, or even a functioning, implementation. I have tried, once the problem is niche enough, the agent just begins to hallucinate solutions that might compile, but don't work the way they should. If your problem is complex enough, verbose enough or simply niche enough, AI can help, but it won't spare you from a genuine knowledge of the intricacies of code, intricacies that you can only get when being close to the metal.

I am very much proud of your work. I genuinely believe that what you are doing is inspiring for juniors and I won't take that from you because you are doing a great job, but don't mistake vibe coding with engineering, because engineering is the only that outlasts scale, vibe coding will keep you stuck.

I am not saying to drop everything in your pipeline because it obviously works for you, but I do encourage you to try to make something without AI, because it will give you understanding of those pieces you never knew you didn't. Even using old code is allowed since it makes you analyze the code from scratch.
